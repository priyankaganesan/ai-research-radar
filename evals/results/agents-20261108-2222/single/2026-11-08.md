## 7. VFlash: Fast, Accurate Visual Planning via Flash Primitive Decoding

**Early buzz** · `2611.08449` · 2026-11-05 · [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08449)

VFlash tackles the high computational cost of autoregressive vision-language models that generate visual plans token-by-token by predicting primitive coordinates jointly instead. The key idea is a lightweight drafter that generates entire primitives in one forward pass while training on annotated structures without requiring exact target-token agreement. The main result shows VFlash reduces prediction errors and achieves approximately 25x faster generation, leading to a 70.2% success rate in robot trajectory tasks compared to the baseline's 62.6%.
