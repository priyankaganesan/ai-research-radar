## Q4. Why doesn't quantizing the KV cache to 2 or 4 bits automatically make decoding faster, and how can that be fixed?

Answered in 100.8s.

**Answer:**

> Quantizing the KV cache to 2 or 4 bits does not automatically speed up decoding because existing fused kernels suffer from severe throughput bottlenecks; specifically, converting each value and applying its scale and offset before feeding Tensor Cores consumes scarce instruction-issue throughput on modern GPUs, causing 2-bit kernels to reach only under 31% of peak HBM throughput [2611.09722]. This issue can be fixed by using **affine folding** to apply scales and offsets to the other operand or output of matrix products rather than per-value, which reduces these operations by up to two orders of magnitude while preserving exact arithmetic results [2611.09722]. Additionally, system-level optimizations like removing the softmax bottleneck via transposed softmax with fused probability quantization can further mitigate accuracy-oriented algorithmic overheads that degrade throughput [2611.09825].
